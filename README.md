# Payroll Fraud Risk Investigation — GRC

## Case Study Architecture

**Real-World Payroll Investigation | Maharashtra, India**

This case study applies a two-stage professional assessment framework:

### 01 — CFE / Fraud Risk Investigation

**Red Flag Identification**  
↓  
**Fraud Hypothesis**  
↓  
**Evidence Collection**  
↓  
**Payroll Reconstruction**  
↓  
**Independent Verification**  
↓  
**Fraud Hypothesis Testing**  
↓  
**Fraud Disposition**

### 02 — GRC / Compliance Assessment

**Regulatory Review**  
↓  
**Control Assessment**  
↓  
**Risk Classification**  
↓  
**Compensation Transparency Assessment**  
↓  
**Control Weakness Identification**  
↓  
**Control Recommendation**

---

---

## Assessment Framework

| Stage | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Investigation** | Fraud risk, financial misconduct, improper deduction, misrepresentation |
| **Evidence Analysis** | Payroll records and independent statutory records |
| **Fraud Assessment** | Test and disposition of fraud hypothesis |
| **Compliance Review** | PF, ESI, tax and wage-structure assessment |
| **GRC Assessment** | Governance, controls, transparency and employee expectation risk |
| **Final Finding** | Fraud disposition + GRC classification |
| **Control Response** | Recommended payroll presentation improvement |
